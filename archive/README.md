# Archive

Everything here is superseded and kept for history. None of it is part of the current pipeline, which
lives in `scripts/` and `src/`, and the paper in `paper/` is the one record of what was measured.

- `notes/` holds the dated lab notes written during the season, plus the old roadmap (`ROADMAP.md`) and
  the early documentation (`documentation.md`). They were rewritten in plain prose in October, with
  their numbers left as they were. Where a note and the paper disagree, the paper is right.
- `scripts/` holds one-off scripts from earlier stages. That covers the first pipeline versions
  (`run_baseline.py`, `run_ttt.py`, `run_ttt_airv.py`), benchmark and profiling probes, TTT diagnostics,
  the llama.cpp LoRA-bridge gates (`ttt_gate1.py` to `ttt_gate3.py`), the DSL and TRM experiments, the
  older notebook builders, and the PowerShell orchestrators that were replaced by Python ones that
  survive a laptop disconnect.
- `notebooks/` holds the submission notebooks from before v7.
- `paper-drafts/` holds the August HTML snapshot of the paper with the equation images it loads, the
  earlier writeup drafts, the outline, and the cover and card designs that were tried and dropped.
